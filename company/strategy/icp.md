# Ideal Customer Profile (canonical)

Single source of truth for the customer. Every skill, outbound rule, lead
score, and piece of copy reads from this file — never restate or reinterpret
the ICP elsewhere; reference this one.

## Who it is

Describe the single buyer you serve: their role, the size of operation they
run, the recurring operational pain they feel, and the outcome they already
pay for. One buyer, stated plainly.

## Who it is NOT

List the audiences you deliberately exclude and why. Exclusions are load-
bearing: every skill and rule below trusts them.
