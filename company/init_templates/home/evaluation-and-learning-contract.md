# Evaluation and learning contract

Every workflow candidate must pin its execution, baseline, rubric and representative, unseen and negative cases. Evaluate actual outputs. Keep engineering, creative quality, external permission and business impact separate. Missing judgment blocks qualification; independent acceptance cannot be supplied by the producer.

Record scoped owner feedback and preserve rejection cases. A lesson requires evaluated same-workflow runs and a later retention check. Saving feedback alone is not learning.

Use `/report` for evidence, memory, actual supplied context, current documents and telemetry. Record only actual exposed usage; source and label reference-price estimates separately from billed costs. Keep owner content in this home, source code in the product repository, and sending/publishing separately approved.
