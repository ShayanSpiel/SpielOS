# Company measurement

The company runtime owns goals, runs, evidence, decisions, and evaluations.

## Evidence classes

- `business` — real market or operational evidence suitable for a business decision.
- `technical_only` — proves machinery, rendering, delivery, or integration behavior.
- `contaminated` — collected during a run whose business interpretation was compromised.
- `invalid` — cannot support the claimed conclusion.

Generated drafts, previews, renders, and successful sends are artifacts or
technical evidence. Replies, qualified conversations, booked calls, sales,
search performance, and attributed conversions can be business evidence when
their source and scope are valid.

Every Department must declare its supported metrics, approval points, external
actions, evidence sources, and evaluation behavior before it becomes production-ready.
