# SpielOS positioning

## Positioning statement

**We help your business to break free from you.**

One sentence. It drives every service, offer, page, and piece of content we
publish. Everything we sell answers one question: how does this make the owner
less necessary to the daily operation of their own business — without losing
control of quality or decisions?

If a service, feature, or sentence cannot be traced back to this sentence, it
does not belong in our offer. Apply the `focus.md` subtraction pass to
positioning itself before adding anything new.

## Category

SpielOS is a harness for operating AI work as a company system: roles, skills,
workflows, context, approvals, evidence, and evaluation connected through one
durable operating loop.

## Buyer-facing promise

Start with one repetitive, expensive workflow. Build a supervised system that
can observe the work, make bounded decisions, act through approved tools, and
show evidence of the result. Repeat until the business runs without the owner
in the loop.

## Commercial paths

Verified against the live site (2026-08-24): `/services/`, `/pricing/`,
`/apply/`.

- **AI implementation services** for established non-technical operators — two
  services, ONE monthly subscription ($2,990/month):
  - **AI Workers** — turn repetitive team work into workflows and agents that
    run without supervision. Break free from *doing*.
  - **DeSlopping** — repair buggy, bloated AI-built software so it stops
    demanding human babysitting. Break free from *fixing*.
- **Entry path**: Free Review via `/apply/` — scope and acceptance criteria
  agreed before any payment. Deal structure: agree the result → we build, you
  verify → if it misses the agreed result, we work until it is fixed.
- **Agent Brief** assessment for mapping the suitable first workflow.
- **SpielOS product adoption** when the buyer is ready to operate the system
  themselves.

## Guardrails

- Never claim complete autonomy or instant company-wide transformation.
- Never treat generated output as proof of business impact.
- Never target builders, AI agencies, or technical harness audiences as the
  primary buyer; the canonical exclusions live in `icp.md`.
- Prefer a measurable operational outcome over feature inventory.
- Every offer must visibly answer "how does this break the business free from
  its owner?" Metric-framed headlines (e.g. "2x your output") may support the
  claim but may not replace the break-free promise.
