# Short-form template research

This durable brief maps the Design Department's registered archetypes to
source-backed visual patterns. It travels with the Department package; no
private runtime artifact is required to validate or use the registry.

## Sources

- [YouTube Shorts creative guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697) — mobile-first vertical composition, early attention, and concise creative.
- [Google ABCD creative principles](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/video/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/) — attract, brand, connect, and direct as a practical creative sequence.
- [W3C WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) — minimum readable contrast for text-led templates.

The registry's `research_map` field records the pattern used by each
archetype. Any new archetype must include an existing HTML template and a
specific mapping before registration.
