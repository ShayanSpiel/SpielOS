Explain the universal vocabulary—Goal, Department, Workflow, Agent, Skill,
Connection, Artifact—and the commands `/start`, `/stop`, `/status`, `/approve`,
and `/help`. Legacy terms are accepted only at migration and persisted-state
boundaries. Mention that Escape cancels the current OpenCode response while
`/stop` persistently disables company automation.
