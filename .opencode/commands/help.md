Explain the universal vocabulary—Goal, Workgroup, Workflow, Worker, Skill,
Connection, Artifact—and the commands `/start`, `/stop`, `/status`, `/approve`,
and `/help`. Department is accepted owner-language for Workgroup; Agent and
Employee are accepted owner-language for Worker. Mention that Escape cancels the current OpenCode response while
`/stop` persistently disables company automation.
